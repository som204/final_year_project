Executive Summary
AY 2024–25 highlights

BIT achieved a historic placement rate of 94.7%, driven by 284 companies visiting campus, 48 new first-time recruiters, and a surge in dream company offers. Research output grew by 18.6% with ₹24.6 Cr in grants secured.

NIRF 2025 ranking improved to #18 — a jump of 5 positions — validating progress across all five NIRF evaluation parameters. Faculty PhD ratio improved to 78%, and 2 patents were granted internationally.
