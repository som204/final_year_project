Executive Summary

The academic year 2024–25 marks a landmark year for NIT, recording the highest placement rate (94.7%) in institutional history. Over 6,800 students across 12 departments engaged in rigorous academic and co-curricular activities.

Research output surged by 18.6%, with 318 peer-reviewed publications and 42 patents filed. The institute secured ₹24.6 Cr in research funding from DST, DRDO, and industry partners.

🏆 NIT ranked #18 in NIRF 2025, a jump of 5 positions from the previous year, reflecting improvements across all five evaluation parameters.
