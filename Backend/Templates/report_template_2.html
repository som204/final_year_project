Executive Summary
Highlights of Academic Year 2024–25

SUT recorded exemplary progress across all institutional parameters in AY 2024–25. The placement rate reached an all-time high of 94.7%, with average CTC improving to ₹18.4 LPA. Research output grew by 18.6%, with 318 publications in reputed journals including 168 SCI-indexed papers.

NIRF 2025 ranked SUT at #18, reflecting sustained improvement across Teaching-Learning, Research, Graduation Outcomes, Outreach, and Perception parameters.
