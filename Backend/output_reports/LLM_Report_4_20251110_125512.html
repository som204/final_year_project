Executive Summary

The 2022-2023 annual report for the University of Toronto (University), covering departmental financial performance, student academics, career placements, and faculty composition. This summary highlights the institution's operational health through budget allocations and revenue, alongside key student outcomes such as academic achievement and employment success with top companies.

Key highlights:

  • Total operating revenue of $1,015,000 with an operating surplus of $15,000.
  • Successfully served a total student body of 6,500, supported by 151 faculty members.
  • Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) was the top-performing department with a surplus of $30,000.
  • Positive change in net position of $30,000, ensuring a solid foundation for future investments.

Placements & Career Services

The academic year demonstrated significant progress in student placements, with graduates securing positions across a diverse range of industries. The placement outcomes reflect a robust average Cost to Company (CTC) of 14.18 LPA. The highest package offered was an exceptional 28.8 LPA, secured by a student from the Information Technology department with Capgemini. A department-wise analysis reveals strong performance across all engineering disciplines. The Information Technology and Computer Science Engineering departments led the placements with the highest salary packages. The recruitment season witnessed participation from numerous leading multinational corporations including Capgemini, Amazon, Samsung, TCS, IBM, and Microsoft.

Faculty Accomplishments

The academic staff profile for the Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) departments reflects a strong foundation of qualified and experienced educators. The faculty is well-credentialed, with a total of 6 Ph.D. holders, 9 M.Tech degree holders, 8 with an M.Sc, and 5 holding a B.Tech across both departments. An analysis of faculty experience reveals a significant depth of professional and academic expertise, with departmental averages around 13 years. The presence of senior faculty members with two decades of experience provides invaluable mentorship and stability.

Strategic Initiatives & Development

Research, infrastructure, and future goals.
Section 3

Research & Publications

The institution has demonstrated a firm commitment to advancing research and scholarly activities through a dedicated internal research funding allocation of ₹120,000 for the fiscal period. This internal budget serves as a primary catalyst for initiating and sustaining academic inquiry across various disciplines. In addition to this institutional support, research efforts are further augmented by external funding secured through direct grants, which contribute significantly to the overall research ecosystem. A structured methodology is in place for managing the financial aspects of externally funded projects, ensuring a sustainable cycle of support for faculty and their scholarly pursuits.

Infrastructure Development

The institution has demonstrated a strong commitment to enhancing its physical campus and learning environment through a dedicated Capital Expenditure Budget of ₹75,000. This allocation was earmarked for strategic upgrades and the acquisition of new assets. Maintaining the existing campus infrastructure to a high standard remains a key operational priority, with a total of ₹90,000 allocated for Facilities, Maintenance & Utilities. The prudent management of both capital investment and ongoing maintenance expenditure ensures the long-term sustainability and enhancement of the institution's physical resources.

Future Goals & Vision

Building on the performance of the 2022-2023 academic year, our primary strategic initiative for the upcoming year is to elevate academic outcomes, particularly for foundational programmes. Our vision for academic excellence is intrinsically linked to the expansion of our research capabilities, with a key objective to significantly increase the volume and value of secured research grants. To enhance the overall student experience, we will focus on integrating academic support with holistic development, including launching mentorship programmes and strengthening advisory frameworks to guide students, especially during their critical first year.
