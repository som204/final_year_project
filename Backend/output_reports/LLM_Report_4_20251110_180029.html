Narrative Analysis

Financial Overview

The academic year demonstrated a robust financial position, culminating in a positive change in net position of ₹30,000. Total operating revenue reached ₹1,015,000, with primary drivers being Tuition and Fees (₹425,000) and Government Grants (₹280,000). Operating expenses totalled ₹1,000,000, resulting in an operating surplus of ₹15,000. All academic departments operated with a surplus, with the CSE department being the top performer.

Academic Performance

The academic year's enrollment figures indicate a robust student body of 6,500 distributed across five key departments. The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) department is the largest with 2200 students, followed by Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) with 1600 students. Analysis of student-to-faculty ratios reveals variations across departments, with CSE at approximately 52:1 and Chemical Engineering at 32:1.

Narrative Analysis

Placements & Career Services

The placement season witnessed robust engagement from leading corporations like Amazon, IBM, and Google. The highest salary package offered was 28.8 LPA. The overall average CTC was approximately 15.32 LPA, indicating strong financial outcomes for graduates. Data suggests recruiters employ a holistic evaluation, as top packages were secured by students with a diverse range of CGPAs.

Research & Publications

The institution secured ₹150,000 in revenue from Research Grants and Sponsored Programs, with a total expenditure of ₹110,000 on direct research activities. An internal Research Funding Allocation of ₹120,000 was also budgeted to provide seed funding for new projects and support ongoing investigations, empowering faculty to pursue innovative research.

Narrative Analysis

Faculty Accomplishments

The institution is supported by 151 faculty members across five key departments, with the CSE department being the largest contingent (42 members). The faculty profile is distinguished by high qualifications, including a significant number of Ph.D. holders, and considerable professional experience ranging from 5 to over 20 years. This blend of credentials and experience enriches the learning environment.

Infrastructure Development

A dedicated capital expenditure budget of ₹75,000 was allocated for strategic projects to upgrade physical and technological assets. In parallel, ₹90,000 was allocated for Facilities, Maintenance, and Utilities to ensure a safe and efficient campus environment. These investments serve as a foundation for future goals, including the expansion of academic spaces and integration of next-generation technology.
