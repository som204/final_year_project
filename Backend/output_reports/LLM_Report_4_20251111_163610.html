Academic Performance & Placements

Section: Academics

Student & Placement Metrics

Academic Performance Summary

The institution's academic landscape is characterized by a robust student body of 6,500 individuals distributed across five primary departments. The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) department is the largest, with an enrollment of 2,200 students, followed by Electrical and Communication Engineering (ECE) with 1,600 students. An analysis of academic achievement reveals a diverse range of performance levels, with a notable segment of high achievers present. The institution caters to both undergraduate and graduate levels of study, reflecting a comprehensive educational structure.

Placements & Career Services Summary

The academic year demonstrated significant progress in student placements, reflecting robust industry engagement. The highest salary package secured was an impressive 28.8 LPA, with the average and median packages standing at 15.32 LPA and 13.0 LPA, respectively. A diverse portfolio of leading corporations recruited from the campus, including prominent technology firms such as Google, Amazon, and IBM. Department-wise analysis reveals exceptional performance across several disciplines, with the Information Technology department leading with the highest offer. This successful placement season is a direct result of the comprehensive support provided by the institution's Career Services.

Faculty Accomplishments

The institution's academic staff comprises a robust and diverse team of educators. An analysis of faculty qualifications reveals a strong academic foundation, with 25% of the faculty holding a Ph.D. and over 52% possessing Master's degrees. The faculty's professional experience is a cornerstone of the institution's strength, with a wide spectrum ranging from 3 to 20 years. This blend of seasoned veterans and dynamic younger academics creates a vibrant educational environment.

Research & Publications

The institution's research endeavours were significantly bolstered by robust external funding. A total of ₹430,000 was secured through external sources, comprising ₹280,000 from Government Grants & Contracts and an additional ₹150,000 from Research Grants & Sponsored Programs. Complementing this, a dedicated internal Research Funding Allocation of ₹120,000 was provisioned. The total operational expenditure on research activities for the period amounted to ₹110,000, indicating a strategic and effective utilisation of allocated resources.

Strategic Outlook & Development

Future Goals, Infrastructure, and Achievements
Section: Strategy

Future Goals & Vision

Building upon the financial framework of the 2022-2023 fiscal year, our strategic plan is centered on optimising our core revenue streams. A primary objective is to enhance institutional support for faculty research initiatives to grow direct grant receipts. Concurrently, we will focus on strengthening enrollment in key disciplines to maximise revenue. The long-term vision is to create a self-sustaining cycle of growth and academic excellence, reinvesting revenue growth into upgrading our campus infrastructure, including laboratories and learning technologies.

Infrastructure Development

The institution has earmarked a significant Capital Expenditure Budget of ₹75,000 for strategic infrastructure upgrades and the development of new facilities. This investment is complemented by a robust commitment to the upkeep of existing assets, with an allocation of ₹90,000 towards Facilities, Maintenance & Utilities. This dual focus on a capital budget for new projects and an operational budget for maintenance reflects a comprehensive and sustainable approach to campus development, ensuring our facilities remain conducive to learning and innovation.

Student Achievements

The academic year demonstrated significant progress in student performance, with several individuals achieving exceptional scholastic merit. These academic accomplishments have translated into outstanding professional opportunities, with students securing positions in leading multinational corporations like Capgemini, IBM, Google, and Amazon. Student achievements were well-distributed across various engineering disciplines. In parallel, the institution continued its commitment to making education accessible through financial aid and scholarships awarded to deserving students.
